Manual checks (not automatic — try these yourself in Safari)

The automatic tests above can't tap buttons on your iPad. These still need a person:

  • Download a backup in the app and find it in the Files app → Downloads.
  • Restore that file: check the confirmation message, tap Cancel, then restore for real.
  • Open a room, tick a chore, reload the page, and check it's still ticked.
  • Use ‹ and › to visit other weeks, then tap "This week" to come back.
  • Move a laundry load through all four steps, then mark it put away.
  • Tap Finish Cleaning Week, choose Carry Over or Done, check the summary, and confirm.
  • Add a note to a chore (More → Add note) and find it in Heads up details and History.
  • On Supplies, open and close categories, add a supply to a category, and move one with Edit.
  • In a room, open a frequency group under All chores, and use More → Edit chore on a checklist item.
  • Check every button is easy to tap and the text is easy to read.
